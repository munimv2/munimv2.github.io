Paste-Safe

Mask client names before you paste into a cloud AI. Put them back in the answer.

An open-source name-finding model (bert-base-NER, MIT) runs in this browser tab. Your text is not uploaded: the page downloads the model once and then works on your device. Check it yourself: open your browser's network tab, or switch off Wi-Fi after the model loads.

Read before you paste. Automatic detection misses things: unusual names, names written in lower case, details described in words. Paste-Safe lowers the risk; it does not make text anonymous, and it is not legal advice about your contracts or data-protection duties. See what it misses on 30 test messages.

Paste-Safe is open source (MIT): github.com/munimv2/paste-safe. Model: Xenova/bert-base-NER (MIT), via Transformers.js (Apache-2.0). The library loads from jsDelivr and the model from Hugging Face; no text goes to either.
